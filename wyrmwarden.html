Three biomes, one dragon

From the forest to Vorthrax's lair

Fight through dungeons

Every level is a dungeon of rooms to clear, with save points, a healing fairy and a boss at the end. The forest is free to play.

Raid tombs and ancient bosses

Mummies, djinn and cacti guard the desert. Pharaoh Sekhmet waits at the end, in one of twelve random skins that each drop their own unique items.

Hollows hide mini-bosses

Side dungeons with their own mini-bosses and better loot. They hit hard and move fast, so bring potions.

Slay the dragon Vorthrax

The frozen mountain ends with Vorthrax, who appears in twelve skins and drops a unique item for each.

Choose your hero

Knight, Mage or Archer

Knight

Shield and sword up close, with a four-branch skill tree that ends in the Warlord line.

Mage

Fire, ice and lightning. Match the element to each enemy's weakness.

Archer

Fight from range and grow through the four-branch Wildcraft line, among others.
