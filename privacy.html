Privacy policy

Effective November 18, 2026

This policy covers the mobile apps published by Stamp Mill Software ("we"), a business of Lodeline Holdings, LLC (formerly Cochran Mineral Co., LLC), a Texas limited liability company. It also covers this website, cochranmineral.com and lodelineholdings.com. If an app handles data differently from what's described here, that app has its own policy, linked from its store listing and from our apps list, and that policy applies instead.

What our apps collect

Our apps don't require an account, and we don't collect your personal information. What you enter in an app is stored on your device. We can't see it, and it isn't sent to our servers.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or share your information.
  • We don't use advertising or tracking in our apps.
  • We don't track you across other apps or websites.

App stores

Apple and Google provide the app stores and may collect data when you download or use an app, such as crash reports, under their own privacy policies.

Deleting your data

Because your data is stored only on your device, deleting the app deletes it.

Children

Our apps aren't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Our websites

Our websites don't use cookies or analytics. If you email us, we use your message and address only to reply.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update it on this page and change the date at the top.

Contact

Questions about privacy: apps@stampmillsoftware.com, or Lodeline Holdings, LLC, Texas, United States.
