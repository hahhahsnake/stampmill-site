Privacy policy

Effective November 21, 2026

This policy covers the mobile apps published by Stamp Mill Software ("we"), a business of Lodeline Holdings, LLC (formerly Cochran Mineral Co., LLC), a Texas limited liability company. It also covers this website, cochranmineral.com and lodelineholdings.com. The sections below apply to our apps in general. Wyrmwarden, our game, includes advertising and in-app purchases and is covered by its own section, which takes priority for that app. If any other app handles data differently, it has its own policy, linked from its store listing and from our apps list.

What our apps collect

Except as described under Wyrmwarden below, our apps don't require an account, and we don't collect your personal information. What you enter in an app is stored on your device. We can't see it, and it isn't sent to our servers.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your information.
  • Our apps don't show ads, use tracking, or share your information with advertisers. The one exception is the free version of Wyrmwarden: its ads are described in the Wyrmwarden section below, which explains what its advertising service collects and how to limit it.
  • Apart from that, we don't track you across other apps or websites.

Purchases

Some apps offer optional purchases, such as Aislewren Pro or a tip. Apple or Google handles the payment. We receive confirmation that a purchase was made so the app can unlock it, not your payment details or name. Purchases that unlock features can be restored on a new phone through your App Store or Google Play account.

Wyrmwarden

Wyrmwarden is a game for iPhone and Android. It has no account or sign-in, and your game progress, settings and characters are stored on your device, not on our servers.

Advertising in the free version

The free version shows ads at save points. Never during boss fights, in dungeons or hollows, in town, or right after your character dies. To show ads, the game uses a third-party advertising service, which may collect information from your device, such as your advertising identifier, IP address, device type and settings, and how you interact with ads. It uses that to deliver and measure ads and to limit fraud. Where the law requires it, the game asks for your consent before showing personalized ads and offers non-personalized ads if you decline. You can also limit ad tracking in your device settings (on iPhone, Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking; on Android, Settings > Privacy > Ads). The Full Quest purchase removes ads entirely.

We will name the advertising service in this policy and in the game's store privacy disclosures before the game is released with ads.

Purchases

Unlocking the Full Quest and buying Marketplace items (expansions and cosmetic skins) are handled by Apple or Google. We receive confirmation that a purchase was made so we can unlock it, not your payment details or name. If you reinstall or change devices, you can restore purchases through your store account.

What we don't do in Wyrmwarden

  • We don't sell your personal information.
  • We don't collect your name, email address, contact list, precise location, photos or microphone data.
  • We don't use your information to build profiles of you.

Children

Wyrmwarden is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, email us and we'll delete it.

App stores

Apple and Google provide the app stores and may collect data when you download or use an app, such as crash reports, under their own privacy policies.

Deleting your data

What you enter in our apps is stored only on your device, so deleting the app deletes it. Information an advertising service collected while showing ads in Wyrmwarden is held by that service under its own policy; the Wyrmwarden section explains how to limit it.

Children

Our apps aren't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Our websites

Our websites don't use cookies or analytics. If you email us, we use your message and address only to reply.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update it on this page and change the date at the top.
