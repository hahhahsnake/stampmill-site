Aislewren

Your price-wise friend in the aisle.

A grocery price book that tells you whether a price is a good deal.

Coming soon to the App Store and Google Play.

Is this a good price?

Type the shelf price and the package size. Aislewren works out the unit price, compares it with what you've paid before at every store, and gives you a clear answer: Stock up, Good deal, Typical or Pricier than usual.

No more package-size math in your head. Grams, ounces, pounds, liters, gallons and counts are all converted for you, so 18 oz for $1.79 and 2 lb for $3.49 compare like for like.

What it does

Your own price book

Track the items you buy most, see the latest price at each store ranked cheapest first, and set a stock-up target for staples.

Shop the cheapest store

Your shopping list is grouped by the store where each item is cheapest, with an estimated total for each store.

Private and offline

No account and no tracking. Everything stays on your phone, and it works without signal.

Free to use. Aislewren Pro is an optional one-time purchase, not a subscription, that adds backup and restore, export to a spreadsheet, and price trend charts for each item.

Privacy
Privacy policy. Aislewren collects no personal data.

Moved to a new phone? Open Aislewren, go to Stores, then Aislewren Pro, and tap Restore purchase. Your price book itself moves with Pro's Back up my data and Restore from a backup.
